GET STARTED / 03

Watch in three steps.

  1. Choose a video.

    Select a file from your device or enter a direct video link.

  2. Pick the video type.

    Normal video for everyday footage. 180° or 360° only for footage recorded in those formats.

  3. Play and explore.

    Drag in panoramic mode, or choose Move phone on compatible devices. VR glasses splits the view for a phone viewer.
